## 1. Sossego-Seeker — pains (well-evidenced)

### Pain 1 — Idle capital + decision paralysis

Money sits unallocated for weeks because no source delivers personalized,
pre-decision *"yes, this fits you specifically"* validation. The public forum
is the makeshift substitute.

> "Tô com um dinheiro parado enquanto não decido minha estratégia" — `sz0ghz`  
> "estou perdido sobre como dar o primeiro passo" — `1figi79`  
> "Me ajude a avaliar a minha carteira?" — `1g182qk`

Reinforced by H4: posture is the dominant axis (45%); the missing piece is
*certainty*, not information.

**Why existing solutions fail.** Brokers offer catalogs not opinions; bank
advisors are commissioned and trust-compromised; robo-advisors give
allocations without the personal "this is right for you" voice.

**Unmet need:** trusted, personalized pre-decision validation — an opinion
the user can act on without forum-asking.

### Pain 2 — First-time IR (income tax) declaration anxiety

Investments come with a tax-reporting obligation the user wasn't front-loaded
about. In April they discover neither the broker nor B3 calculates
automatically; data is fragmented across platforms.

> "Eu também achava que as corretoras e/ou a B3 iriam calcular automaticamente" — `1aopejb`  
> "Procuro essa resposta há um tempo, mas não acho resposta" — `hcu79x`

**Why existing solutions fail.** Informes-de-rendimentos PDFs assume tax
literacy; specialized IR calculators require discovery and don't ingest
broker data.

**Unmet need:** integrated, plain-language IR preparation — broker data →
pre-filled, explained declaration.

### Pain 3 — Property-purchase financial complexity

At first-mortgage moments, terms are opaque, no integrated planning tool
exists, and the system reads as stacked against the buyer.

> "financiamento habitacional é algo abominável aqui" — `10lsoqd`  
> "sou duro e fodido portanto não possuo entrada" — `101x1e6`

**Why existing solutions fail.** Banks compete on advertised rate, not on
transparency or fit; no tool walks a first-time buyer through *"buy now vs.
save longer, given my cashflow?"* neutrally.

**Unmet need:** neutral, transparent property-financing planning — a
fit-and-cashflow analysis, not a sales channel.

## 2. O Sardinha — pains (real, with mandatory wellbeing tension flag)

**⚠ Wellbeing flag — read before reading the pains.** The Sardinha's pains
are genuine, and the speculative cycle visibly produces emotional and
financial harm in the locked quotes ("Tô abalado", "perdi 800 conto"). **Any
product response to this persona must not amplify speculative harm.** A
"trade better" tool fails this guardrail. Solutions for the Sardinha must
reduce harm or open structured pathways *away* from pure speculation — never
optimize the speculation itself. Phase 1's locked ethics rule (observe and
count, never facilitate) applies here as strongly as it does to the
gray-zone behavior in the cynical-reactive persona.

### Pain 1 — Emotional whiplash of the speculative cycle

Oscillation between euphoric conviction and morning-after despair, often in
the same thread. The cycle is *structural*: H2 shows the persona shrinks
monotonically as Selic rises (51 → 33 → 16% A→C) — they don't graduate, they
thin out.

> "OS CARAS VAO POR ESSA MERDA EM 1000 DOLARES" — `l67k4m` (peak)  
> "Tô abalado e vou dormir pra ver se passa" — `1fkjsj4` (despair)  
> "eu tenho loss até quando tenho gain" — `zexdpw` (resignation)

**Why existing solutions fail.** Brokers reward trading volume; communities
normalize the swings; nothing introduces friction at the euphoric peak.

**Unmet need:** non-pathologizing harm-reduction — friction at peaks and
structured off-ramps when the user wants to step back, without demanding
identity-change. **Harder than Sossego-Seeker needs because the product must
not amplify the behavior it serves.**

### Pain 2 — Structurally outgunned, and knows it

Cognitive dissonance: the Sardinha self-identifies against whales and
manipulators, knows the game is stacked, continues to play.

> "Não faço ideia do que tô fazendo." — `lm3va5`

Reinforced by H1 (V=0.68): r/farialimabets is a measurably distinct
population, not the mainstream investor at a different venue — a culture, not
a deficit.

**Why existing solutions fail.** "Education" pretends sophistication can
overcome structure; the Sardinha sees through it. Gurus exploit the same
insight cynically.

**Unmet need:** honest framing of retail's structural position + tools that
work *within* those constraints, not promises to transcend them.

## 3. O Cético Irônico — pains (partly inferred, lower-confidence)

**⚠ Confidence flag — read before reading the pains.** This persona's
defining behaviour is **non-disclosure**: 67–80% `desconhecido` on situational
fields (institutional relationship, accumulation phase, financial goal). They
perform commentary rather than describe pains directly. The pains below are
**inferred** from the ironic register, the exclusion-beneath-the-irony locked
quote, the H1/H2 findings, and the Phase 1 corpus-level observation that the
tax gray-zone behavior concentrates in this region. Treat as
**lower-confidence** than the other two personas' pains; in the Phase 4D
memo, signal the interpretive weight honestly.

**⚠ Ethics guardrail (BINDING for Phase 4D and beyond).** The Phase 1 schema
observed `evasao_ou_zona_cinzenta` (tax gray-zone / under-reporting / dubious
offshore structures) as a real coded behavior in the corpus. Cross-tabbed
against M2, that behavior concentrates in this persona's neighborhood — not
as the cluster's defining trait, but as a discernible bias. **The needs
identified below point toward making compliance and legitimacy painless and
trustworthy. They DO NOT facilitate evasion or any gray-zone activity.** The
locked CLAUDE.md ethics rule ("observe and count, never facilitate") must
hold from this document through the final memo.

### Pain 1 — Exclusion: "this isn't for me"

Beneath the irony is genuine exclusion. The 67–80% `desconhecido` rate on
institutional-relationship fields reads as "not engaging the question." The
self-tag names what the irony covers.

> "Mendigo digital" — `1hap814` (exclusion beneath irony)  
> "Sindrome de viralata é foda." — `m8n9gl` (being-left-out as cultural critique)

**Why existing solutions fail.** Mainstream products design for the earnest
learner; their marketing tone is what this persona reads as suspect.

**Unmet need:** credibility-by-design — quiet, no-claims, transparent
offerings. The marketing has to be no-marketing.

### Pain 2 — Distrust of every intermediary

Defining institutional cynicism: gurus, brokers, banks, advisors, the system
itself. The locked quotes mock both the rhetoric of finance influencers and
the worthlessness of pitched returns.

> "Acordo às 3 da manhã para sair na frente do meu inimigo" — `ugezdu`  
> "Investa R$ 1.000 e depois de 1 mês pegue seus R$ 1.000 de volta!" — `1041y0j`  
> "Perde somente para a poupança..." — `1041y0j`

Reinforced by H4: highest cynicism in corpus (3.09 / 5).

**Why existing solutions fail.** Every new offering looks like another pitch;
polish reads as suspect; endorsements destroy trust.

**Unmet need:** product narratives that don't perform success and don't
recruit ambassadors — offerings that avoid the marketing surface of the
category.

### Pain 3 (ethics-bound) — Tax / legitimacy friction

Compliance reads as punitive and gotcha-heavy; the response is disengagement,
sometimes into gray-zone territory.

**Evidence (corpus-level, not single-quote):** Phase 1 observation of
`evasao_ou_zona_cinzenta` concentrating in this region + high `desconhecido`
on `perfil_tributario_e_fiscal` in the M2-C1 fingerprint. Lower-confidence per
the flag above.

**Why existing solutions fail.** Tax compliance is framed as
obligation-with-penalty, not routine background task.

**Unmet need (ethics-bound — restate guardrail).** Low-friction,
no-moralizing compliance — make legitimacy easier than the gray zone. **NOT
facilitation of evasion or under-reporting.** Remove the friction that pushes
toward gray-zone choices; do not optimize the gray zone.
